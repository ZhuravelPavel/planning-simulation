**In force for isexychat.com:** the November–January sequence in §1, written from the 26 October 2026 status note. New URLs, the blog program, and a new language wait until that sequence is moving.

## 1. ISC, November through January

The goal is to stop the slide, put one URL on each head query, and get `/adult-chat/` chosen when Google is already showing it. Signup and chat-start are not measurable yet. A ranking change cannot be called a win until those events exist, so the measurement work is part of the sequence, not a later nicety.

If time runs out, do the steps in this order and stop: query-to-URL map, the adult-chat title and description, the with-men diagnosis, India rank on “sex chat” and “free sex chat,” then the GA4 key events. Desktop, the blog, and Arabic come after those.

### November — freeze the map

1. Assign one indexable URL to each of these queries and write the assignment down: sex chat, free sex chat, live sex chat, sex video chat, adult chat, porn chat, sex chat with girls, sex chat with men.
2. Start from this map unless a crawl shows it is wrong: `/sex-chat/` owns “sex chat” and “free sex chat.” `/adult-chat/` owns “adult chat.” `/with-women/` and `/with-men/` own the gendered queries. The homepage owns the brand.
3. Point internal anchors at the assigned URL only. Stop sending the same query to a second URL.
4. Rewrite the title and description on `/adult-chat/` first. It already has the impressions. The lever is the snippet, not a new page. The bar to beat is a click-through rate of about 1.5%.
5. Diagnose `/with-men/` before you add rooms. Its impressions fell hard while its average position barely moved, which means queries left the index or moved to another URL. Find which.
6. In GA4, set key events for registration and for a chat actually starting, broken out by landing page and by country. Until those fire, do not report “organic revenue” from GA4. The export shows almost no key events and no revenue.
7. Check why desktop impressions fell. Keep AMP. Confirm the AMP title matches the canonical title on the homepage and on `/sex-chat/`.
8. Add internal links to the four money rooms so `/rules/` and `/general-chat/` are less likely to take the sitelink slots. They rank at the top on large impression counts and almost nobody clicks them.
9. Pick one host for the brand result. `chat.isexychat.com` has been climbing on the brand query and competing with the apex. The other host should be a sitelink, not a second result.

### December — take the head term back

1. Make `/sex-chat/` and the homepage do different jobs. The room page gets a visible free-chat proposition, a short FAQ, and internal links that use the exact head queries. The homepage reads as the brand and the directory, so it stops pulling “free sex chat” onto a URL that ranks worse for it, including in India.
2. Give the video cluster one owner. `/video-chat/` is far off page one, while “sex video chat” and “porn chat” have been losing rank on whichever URL holds them. Either put those queries on `/video-chat/` and link to it from `/sex-chat/`, or keep them on `/sex-chat/` and remove the third competitor. Do not leave both.
3. Use India and the Philippines as the rank checks. Both lost about half a position and both click when they are shown, so a recovery shows up quickly.
4. Pass links to `/sex-chat/` from the homepage, the blog, and the gendered rooms before anyone buys links. The room has a small referring-domain count next to the homepage.
5. Refresh the dirty-text-messages post, which has been moving up, and link it into the room it supports.

### January — extend only what recovered

1. If `/adult-chat/` click-through has lifted, move that page toward the top five with the same internal-link pattern used on `/sex-chat/`. If it has not lifted, do not add pages around it. Fix the snippet again.
2. If `/with-men/` impressions are still near a million, rebuild that template. Do not add more rooms on top of a template that lost its queries.
3. Lesbian chat and teen chat are the next pages to push onto page one, after the head terms, because they already improved.
4. Review the keywords Ahrefs marked lost. Restore the ones that were real rankings on the assigned URL. Ignore a brand-query URL that Search Console does not support with clicks. Ahrefs has pointed the US brand query at a mobile room URL that barely gets Search Console clicks. Treat that as sitelink noise.
5. Canada, Egypt, South Africa, and Iran gained clicks through the downturn. Keep their landing pages on the same template as the pages that recover in India.
6. Arabic “سكس شات” is the localization test, and only after the English head terms have stopped falling. One Arabic landing page. Not a sitewide translation.
7. Report the quarter on Search Console clicks, the position of the eight assigned queries, India clicks, adult-chat click-through, and the new organic key-event rate. Use Ahrefs to see which URL is attached to a keyword. Use Search Console for clicks.
